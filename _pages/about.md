# Junteng Liu

First-year PhD candidate at HKUST NLP Group. Research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. Research interests include:

- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM truthfulness and Interpretability

## Academic Background

- **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)** — Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024-Present), HKUST NLP Group (research group at HKUST led by Professor Junxian He; Ph.D. supervisor: Professor Junxian He).
- **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** — B.Eng. (2020-2024), graduated in June 2024. Previously advised by Professor Junxian He during undergraduate studies at SJTU.
- **Honors & Awards** — Received Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship, awarded by Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

## Research Experience

- **MINIMAX** — Research Intern (February 2025 - Present), AI/technology company.
- **Tencent WXG** — Research Intern (June 2024 - September 2024), Division of Tencent (part of Tencent technology company). Advisor: Zifei Shan.
- **Shanghai AI Lab** — Research Intern (June 2023 - December 2023), AI research laboratory. Advisor: Prof. Yu Cheng (associated with Shanghai AI Lab).
